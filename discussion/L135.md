# L135 zkSecurity Renegade atomic settlement #00 (High): transfer amount underflow
Source: reports/linked/zksecurity-renegade-atomic-settlement.html (zkSecurity, Oct 2024).
Relayer fee not bounded in-circuit; contract computes receive_amount - fees unchecked, so a huge relayer fee underflows trader_take. Exploitable only if the protocol holds near U256::MAX of the token (relayer fee routed to the contract itself). Fee and amounts are fixed by the proof; the bug is contract arithmetic plus a missing bound, with an impractical precondition.
Proposal: N, PSS N, M. Alt Y P1 (fee range not enforced).

## Final (rater A, 2026-11-24)
Confirmed: not binding as proposed.
