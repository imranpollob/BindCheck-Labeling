# L187 Matter Labs ZK Email issue 1 (Critical): From header manipulation (Outlook, Mail.ru)
Source: reports/linked/matterlabs-zkemail.pdf (Matter Labs, 2024-10-25). ZK Email: DKIM-verified emails drive on-chain actions (e.g. account recovery); the From address extracted in-circuit is the identity the contract acts on.
Providers DKIM-sign From headers like '"Name <victim@x>" < attacker@outlook.com>'; FromAddrRegex extracts victim@x. The regex spec doesn't match how providers determine the real sender: a wrong constraint (spec) in the circuit; the extracted address is a proved output.
Proposal: N, [circuit-soundness], M. Alt Y OTHER (proved 'From' doesn't mean 'sender').
