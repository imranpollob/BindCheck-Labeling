## Pass 1: report only
The circuit ties chunk data to the L1 blob by evaluating the blob polynomial at a Fiat-Shamir challenge z and comparing y with the contract's KZG point-evaluation. The challenge preimage includes the chunk data but not the KZG commitment/versioned hash (weak Fiat-Shamir). A malicious sequencer can pick several chunks, compute their (z_i, y_i), publish a blob through all those points, and later prove whichever chunk suits them. Bridge scenario: finalize different chunks on two chains.
