## Pass 1: report only
The circuit ties chunk data to the L1 blob by evaluating the blob polynomial at a Fiat-Shamir challenge z and comparing y with the contract's KZG point-evaluation. The challenge preimage includes the chunk data but not the KZG commitment/versioned hash (weak Fiat-Shamir). A malicious sequencer can pick several chunks, compute their (z_i, y_i), publish a blob through all those points, and later prove whichever chunk suits them. Bridge scenario: finalize different chunks on two chains.
- C1 met, C2 met (batch finalized: state root, withdraw root). C3: the consumer-held blob commitment, z, y and roots are all in the statement; what fails is the soundness of the in-circuit argument that the proven chunk equals the blob's data. A Fiat-Shamir transcript flaw (guide §4 lists FS flaws under PSS).
- Proposed: N, NA, PSS Y, conf M. Arguable Y/OTHER: the finding's own title says the public statement (blob commitment) isn't bound into the challenge, so the data the consumer committed to isn't tied to the data proven. Requires a malicious sequencer. Check in code whether the versioned hash is part of the batch public input.
