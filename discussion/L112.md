# L112 ToB TOB-IDEN3-1 (High): Num2Bits(254/256) aliasing in iden3 circuits
Source: reports/linked/2024-05-polygonlabs-iden3circuits-securityreview.pdf (ToB, fetched 2026-11-24).
Non-unique bit decompositions of private claim fields: revocation nonce (claim[4]+p) bypasses revocation, expiration can be faked, LessThan254 query results wrong. Mechanism is in-circuit on private witness data; the public outputs/inputs the verifier contract checks (issuer state, query, etc.) stay fixed by the proof. Same shape as L047 (Num2Bits(254) SMT).
Proposal: N, [circuit-soundness], M. Duplicate of L121 (same finding, other report copy).
