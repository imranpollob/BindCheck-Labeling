# L043 · FULL · Arianee privacy extension (Veridise 2024-07, sheet vuln 4573fe9; report commit b7da01e) · V-ARIA-VUL-001 "CreditNoteProofs can be stolen" (Critical, fixed 095a366 / f95260c; sheet fix 5451977)
## Pass 1: report only
A CreditNoteProof shows a credit commitment is in the credit Merkle tree and reveals a nullifier; ArianeeIssuerProxy spends it (e.g. updateSmartAsset) alongside an OwnershipProof. The credit proof isn't tied to the spender or the action (no intent), so anyone who sees it in the mempool can front-run and spend the victim's credit on their own action.
- C1 met, C2 met (nullifier marked spent, credit pays for the front-runner's action), C3 met (which action/who the credit pays for isn't fixed by the proof).
- Guide §1 front-running redirect. Fix: add pubIntentHash to creditVerifier (bind to msg.data) like the ownership proof. Proposed: Y, P1 (alt P2 "context/intent not bound"), PSS N, conf H.
